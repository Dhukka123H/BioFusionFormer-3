### Stage A Reconciliation: Divergence Resolution

Two divergent summaries previously existed in repository history (`summary_1.md` and `summary_f41d43.md`) reporting different metrics (size Spearman 0.91 vs 0.85, stability 0.617 vs 0.399, generic fraction 55% vs 40%). Investigation of git history confirms that `summary_1.md` (commit `f929ae7`, Oct 19, `umair1405`) was generated by the complete Stage A addendum pipeline: it used the 5 fold models from Stage 3 (`experiments/final_v1/E1/folds/`), correctly stripped the 726 orphaned miRNA fallback edges from pathway token $P-1=191$ while aggregating genuine Reactome edges across all three modalities (mRNA, miRNA, methylation), evaluated all 3 matched null permutations (`null_permutations/perm_0*.json`), grouped overlapping pathways into independent clusters using pairwise gene-set Jaccard $\ge 0.5$, compared against a size-normalised ElasticNet baseline, generated descriptive feature rankings, and audited per-repeat calibration. In contrast, `summary_f41d43.md` (commit `5cba576`, Oct 21, `Dhukka123H`) was produced by an earlier development script that failed to parse the null cache, omitted redundancy clustering, feature views, and calibration audits, and reported obsolete pre-fix numbers with ODC simply removed while incorrectly asserting that size bias was "removed" (despite a significant Spearman $\rho = 0.29, p = 3.6e-5$). Therefore, the Stage A addendum pipeline (`f929ae7`) and its cached data (`explainability_results.json`) represent the single authoritative ground truth.

## 1. Technical Fallback Audit (ODC Artefact)

In the frozen E1 architecture, 726 miRNA features without a prior edge were wired into the last selected pathway token ($P-1$), which happened to be **Regulation of ornithine decarboxylase (ODC)** (`R-HSA-350562`). This artificial concentration of 726 features caused ODC to falsely appear as the #1 attributed pathway in earlier drafts. **ODC is confirmed to be an artefact of the fallback wiring.** When fallback edges are excluded, attributions are dominated by pathway size (Spearman 0.91) and by translation/ribosome/splicing genes; after size normalisation stability is not above the matched null.

- **Pooled Single-Repeat Slope:** **1.731** (95% patient bootstrap CI: `[1.352, 2.286]`)
- **Pooled Single-Repeat Intercept:** **-0.271** (95% patient bootstrap CI: `[-0.719, 0.202]`)

> **Clinical Deployment Rule & GUI Verification:** Because single-repeat slopes (ranging from 1.434 to 1.974, with repeats 3, 4, and 5 falling below 1.769) are not strictly consistent within the repeat-averaged CI [1.769, 3.154], deployment interfaces (GUI and CLI) must display raw predicted scores with an explicit **"Not Calibrated"** label rather than claiming a calibrated probability. The final deployment model was trained on the full cohort without cross-validation, so its calibration cannot be directly evaluated in deployment; cross-validated Brier score improvements (0.011–0.016 across individual repeats and 0.015 pooled) demonstrate the potential of Platt scaling and are documented as prospective future work.
